# aether-ai
Autonomous Intelligence for Space Exploration - Your mission. AI's navigation. Infinite possibilities.

Aether AI is an autonomous space exploration platform where users describe missions in natural language and AI agents plan, simulate, and execute them using physics-based models.

From interplanetary travel and precision landings to rotating spacecraft docking, black-hole flybys, and Mars rover exploration, Aether AI transforms mission objectives into autonomous actions.

Powered by frontier LLMs, orbital mechanics, and interactive 3D simulations, Aether AI can adapt to changing conditions, optimize trajectories, manage spacecraft resources, and solve unexpected challenges in real time.

The vision: Don't pilot the spacecraft. Tell it where you want to go.
